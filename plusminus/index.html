Troubleshooting & Good to know

Safe to Spend looks higher or lower than expected: Make sure Current money matches your current balance, and review unpaid or overdue expenses in Live. Scheduled future income before your next primary payday is included automatically, while overdue and due-today income counts once marked received. Tap the info button beside Safe to Spend for the Spending calculation, or see Settings → Understanding PlusMinus → How Calculations Work.

Avoiding double-deductions when marking expenses paid: When you tap Mark Paid, PlusMinus asks whether to Subtract from Current Money or keep your balance unchanged because Current Money already reflects this. If you update your balance manually first, quick reconciliation chips let you mark settled expenses without subtracting them twice, and Undo lets you reverse a mistake immediately.

Backups and moving to another device: PlusMinus stores all budget data locally on your device. To move your budget or keep a copy, open Settings → Data & Privacy → Backup & Restore and tap Create Backup to save a .plusminusbackup file via Files. Restoring a backup replaces all current budget data and restores the balance as it was when the backup was created, while keeping your app language and appearance unchanged.
