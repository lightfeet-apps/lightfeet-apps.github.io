Troubleshooting & Good to know

Safe to Spend looks higher or lower than expected: Make sure Current Available Money reflects your real balance right now, and review any unpaid or overdue bills in Live. Scheduled future recurring income arriving before your next primary payday is automatically factored into Safe to Spend using the tightest cash-flow point (without letting you spend money before it arrives), while one-time, overdue, and due-today income waits until you mark it received. You can also inspect the full calculation rules in Settings → How Calculations Work.

Avoiding double-deductions when marking bills paid: When you tap Mark Paid, PlusMinus asks whether to Subtract from Current Money or keep your balance unchanged because Current Money already reflects this. If you update your balance manually first, quick reconciliation chips let you mark settled bills without subtracting them twice, and Undo lets you reverse a mistake immediately.

Backups and moving to another device: PlusMinus stores all budget data locally on your device. To move your budget or keep a copy, open Settings → Data & Privacy → Backup & Restore and tap Create Backup to save a .plusminusbackup file via Files. Restoring a backup replaces all current budget data and restores the balance as it was when the backup was created, while keeping your app language unchanged.
