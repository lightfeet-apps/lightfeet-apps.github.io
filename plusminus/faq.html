How is Safe to Spend calculated?

Live answers one practical question: How much money can I safely spend right now until my next primary payday? It starts from a clear formula and automatically handles scheduled upcoming income:

  • Current money: Your manually maintained checking or cash balance right now, shown with a relative last-updated timestamp.
  • Minus unpaid expenses: Unpaid recurring expenses and one-time expenses due before your next primary payday, plus any overdue unpaid expenses from the immediately preceding pay period.
  • Minus remaining food estimate: Your estimated remaining food budget for the active cash-flow horizon.
  • Tightest cash-flow point for upcoming income: When scheduled future income (recurring or one-time) arrives before your next primary payday, PlusMinus checks your balance before each upcoming income date and through the end of the cycle, picking the tightest point so later expenses don't cause false shortages while you never spend money before it arrives. Tap the info button beside Safe to Spend to open Spending calculation, which always matches that limiting horizon line by line (showing Income added to this calculation, Reserved for expenses before [date], Food estimate, and any Later unpaid expenses), while the hero overline clearly reads SAFE TO SPEND · CONSIDERING UPCOMING INCOME whenever upcoming income is factored in.
Tip: You can review the calculation rules at any time inside the app under Settings → Understanding PlusMinus → How Calculations Work.

What is the difference between Live and Plan?

PlusMinus separates day-to-day spending awareness from your long-term monthly baseline across two distinct tabs:

  • Live: Covers right now until your next primary payday. It starts from your real Current money, evaluates unpaid expenses, remaining food, and any future scheduled incomes across the tightest cash-flow point, and tells you your current Safe to Spend alongside expandable Upcoming income and Unpaid expenses lists.
  • Plan: Shows your steady-state Planned Monthly Remainder (normalized monthly income minus normalized monthly expenses minus your monthly food budget), completely independent of today's bank balance or payday cycle length. It also includes Where your income goes and Plan analysis, comparing your monthly allocation across categories and your food budget as amounts and shares of monthly income.
Why both views? A yearly insurance expense or shorter February pay period affects how much cash you can spend right now in Live, while Plan tells you whether your normal income covers your regular expenses over time.

How does the Food Budget work, and can I override it for the current period?

Instead of tracking every grocery receipt, PlusMinus models food as a single monthly estimate that automatically scales down as payday approaches:

  • Automatic daily proration: PlusMinus calculates Monthly Food Budget × (days remaining until next payday ÷ total days in the current payday cycle), rounded up to a whole currency unit. Today is included and payday is excluded.
  • Custom period override: In Live, tap Food estimate to set a fixed amount for the current period (including zero) if you already bought groceries in bulk or are traveling. Your monthly food budget in Plan stays unchanged.
  • Restoring automatic calculation: Open the Food estimate sheet and tap Restore automatic calculation at any time, or let it reset automatically on your next primary payday.
Note: Secondary income dates never reset your food budget — only your Primary Payday anchors and resets the food estimate.

How does PlusMinus prevent deducting the same expense twice?

Because you update your balance manually without a bank connection, PlusMinus includes built-in safeguards so paid expenses never reduce Safe to Spend twice:

  • Two-button confirmation on Mark Paid: When marking an expense paid, PlusMinus asks whether to Subtract from Current Money or keep your balance as-is because Current Money already reflects this (with smart emphasis if you updated your balance within the last 5 minutes).
  • Quick reconciliation chips: After a manual balance update, quick chips let you mark recently due expenses as settled without subtracting them from your balance again.
  • Safe Undo: If you tap the wrong option, tap Undo right away to restore the expense and any automatic balance change cleanly.

How does upcoming income affect Safe to Spend?

PlusMinus works cleanly whether you have one main paycheck, 2–3 staggered recurring incomes, or a scheduled future one-time payment:

  • Future scheduled income (automatic cash-flow bottleneck): Active recurring and one-time income scheduled strictly after today (and after the current cycle's nominal primary payday when weekend shifting applies) and before your next primary payday is automatically factored into Safe to Spend via the tightest checkpoint — preventing false shortages from later expenses while never letting you spend money before it arrives.
  • Clear labels and exact breakdown: When upcoming income raises Safe to Spend above cash on hand alone, the hero overline updates to SAFE TO SPEND · CONSIDERING UPCOMING INCOME (with a Full period through … footnote below when the limiting point ends earlier). Tap the info button for Spending calculation to inspect Income added to this calculation (distinct from the overview's total Upcoming income) and any Later unpaid expenses covered by later income.
  • Overdue and due-today income: Overdue and due-today incomes (both recurring and one-time) are never projected ahead of receipt — they appear as reminder rows and count only once marked Received (or reflected in Current money). You can also mark a missed income as Income cancelled without altering your balance.
  • No double-counting when received: Marking an included upcoming income Received and adding it to Current money moves it cleanly into your balance without double-counting.

How do Custom Months, Yearly, Weekly, Biweekly, One-Time items, and Plan analysis work?

PlusMinus handles regular expenses, irregular schedules, and category breakdowns accurately across Plan and Live:

  • Plan normalization: Weekly items normalize via × 52 ÷ 12, every 2 weeks via × 26 ÷ 12, Custom Months (such as January & October or Quarterly presets) via × selected months ÷ 12, and yearly items via ÷ 12.
  • Live full deductions: Yearly and custom-month expenses are deducted in full during the actual pay period when they fall due, so you see their real cash impact before payday.
  • One-Time items: One-time income and one-time expenses (for example a tax refund or car repair) have an exact date and can be added in Plan or via the + menu in Live. They are excluded from your steady-state Plan baseline: one-time expenses deduct in full when due in Live, while future scheduled one-time income before next payday enters the Safe to Spend cash-flow check without spending it early.
  • 17 categories, Plan analysis & optional due days: Expenses can be grouped into 17 optional categories (Housing, Utilities, Subscriptions, Insurance, Loans, Credit Cards & Consumer Credit, Transport, Hobbies, Sports & Fitness, Investing, Health & Wellbeing, Children & Family, Pets, Education, Saving, Giving, Other). On Plan, Where your income goes and Plan analysis compare active recurring expense categories (plus Uncategorized and Food Budget) by monthly amount and share of monthly income. Recurring expenses can also omit a specific due day (No specific day) if needed.

How do Primary Payday and weekend payday shifting work?

Your Primary Payday in Settings → Budget Cycle anchors your pay cycles and food estimate resets:

  • Days 1–31 with short-month handling: Choose your main payday from day 1 to 31. In shorter months (such as February or 30-day months), PlusMinus automatically clamps the date to the last day of that month.
  • Weekend shifting: Turn on Pay on Friday if payday falls on a weekend if your employer pays on the preceding Friday whenever the payday falls on Saturday or Sunday.
  • Cycle preview: In the Primary Payday picker, you can preview upcoming effective payday dates before saving.

What are Coming Next Period, paying expenses early, and older unsettled expenses?

PlusMinus makes it easy to stay ahead of upcoming cycles and keep older periods tidy:

  • Coming Next Period & early payment: Expand Coming Next Period in Live to preview expenses due after your next primary payday. If you already paid a next-period expense early, mark it paid there with the same balance consistency check.
  • Older unsettled expenses (Mark older settled): Unpaid expenses from before the previous cycle (up to 24 months old) are excluded from deducting against your current Safe to Spend and surfaced in a non-destructive catch-up banner with Mark older settled.

How are amounts rounded?

Every visible figure is designed so the arithmetic adds up clearly:

  • Cent precision & exact totals: Monetary components are rounded to the nearest cent (with half-cent ties rounding away from zero) before summing or subtracting, so every displayed line adds up to the exact total shown.
  • Food estimate rounding: The automatic daily food estimate in Live rounds up to a whole currency unit, whereas custom fixed food estimates keep cents.
  • Clean decimal display: Amounts with cents display two decimal places, while whole amounts omit trailing decimals.

Does PlusMinus connect to my bank, collect analytics, or sync automatically to the cloud?

No. PlusMinus is built strictly as a local-first, zero-tracking app:

  • Stored on your device: Balances, incomes, expenses, payment history, and preferences stay locally on your iPhone or iPad.
  • No accounts or bank links: PlusMinus requires no account and never asks for bank credentials or Open Banking access.
  • Zero tracking or ads: There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no automatic cloud sync in the background.

How do Backup & Restore and Reset All Data work?

You stay in full control of your local budget data at all times:

  • Create Backup: Open Settings → Data & Privacy → Backup & Restore and tap Create Backup to export a readable JSON archive (.plusminusbackup, not password-protected) to iCloud Drive, local storage, or any location in Files.
  • Restore from Backup: Tap Restore from Backup and select a valid .plusminusbackup file (up to 50 MB). Restoring replaces all current budget data and restores the balance from when the backup was created, while keeping your app language and appearance unchanged.
  • Reset All Data: Under Settings → Data & Privacy, Reset All Data uses a two-step confirmation to erase all local budget data and preferences on this device and return to initial setup. Exported backup files and iOS system backups are not deleted.

What languages, currencies, themes, and devices are supported?

PlusMinus adapts to your preferred language, currency, and appearance:

  • Languages: Switchable directly inside Settings → Preferences → Language between System Default, English, Suomi (Finnish), Svenska (Swedish), Deutsch (German), Español (Spanish), and Français (French) without restarting the app.
  • Currencies: Supports EUR, USD, GBP, SEK, NOK, DKK, CHF, CAD, AUD, and JPY under Settings → Preferences → Currency.
  • Appearance: Choose System Default, Dark, or Light under Settings → Preferences → Appearance.
  • Supported devices: Built for iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or later.
