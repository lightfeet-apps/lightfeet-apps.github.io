How is Safe to Spend calculated?

Live answers one practical question: How much money can I safely spend right now until my next primary payday? It uses a transparent 3-line formula:

  • Current Available Money: Your manually maintained checking or cash balance right now, shown with a relative last-updated timestamp.
  • Minus unpaid bills: Unpaid recurring bills and one-time bills due before your next primary payday, plus any overdue unpaid bills from the immediately preceding pay period.
  • Minus remaining food estimate: Your estimated remaining food budget for the rest of the current payday cycle.
Tip: You can review the calculation breakdown at any time inside the app under Settings → Understanding PlusMinus → How Calculations Work.

What is the difference between Live and Plan?

PlusMinus separates day-to-day spending awareness from your long-term monthly baseline across two distinct tabs:

  • Live: Covers right now until your next primary payday. It starts from your real Current Available Money, deducts bills due in this pay period and remaining food, and tells you your current Safe to Spend.
  • Plan: Shows your steady-state Planned Monthly Remainder (normalized monthly income minus normalized monthly bills minus your monthly food budget), completely independent of today's bank balance or payday cycle length.
Why both views? A yearly insurance bill or shorter February pay period affects how much cash you can spend right now in Live, while Plan tells you whether your normal income covers your regular expenses over time.

How does the Food Budget work, and can I override it for the current period?

Instead of tracking every grocery receipt, PlusMinus models food as a single monthly estimate that automatically scales down as payday approaches:

  • Automatic daily proration: PlusMinus calculates Monthly Food Budget × (days remaining until next payday ÷ total days in the current payday cycle), rounded up to a whole currency unit. Today is included and payday is excluded.
  • Custom period override: In Live, tap Food estimate to set a fixed amount for the current period (including zero) if you already bought groceries in bulk or are traveling. Your monthly food budget in Plan stays unchanged.
  • Restoring automatic calculation: Open the Food estimate sheet and tap Restore automatic calculation at any time, or let it reset automatically on your next primary payday.
Note: Secondary income dates never reset your food budget — only your Primary Payday anchors and resets the food estimate.

How does PlusMinus prevent deducting the same bill twice?

Because you update your balance manually without a bank connection, PlusMinus includes built-in safeguards so paid bills never reduce Safe to Spend twice:

  • Two-button confirmation on Mark Paid: When marking a bill paid, PlusMinus asks whether to Subtract from Current Money or keep your balance as-is because Current Money already reflects this (with smart emphasis if you updated your balance within the last 5 minutes).
  • Quick reconciliation chips: After a manual balance update, quick chips let you mark recently due bills as settled without subtracting them from your balance again.
  • Safe Undo: If you tap the wrong option, tap Undo right away to restore the bill and any automatic balance change cleanly.

Why isn't upcoming income added to Safe to Spend automatically?

PlusMinus keeps Safe to Spend strictly grounded in money you actually have on hand right now:

  • Quiet reminder chip: If another recurring or one-time income is expected before your next primary payday, it appears quietly in Live as an upcoming income reminder so you know it is on the way.
  • Never inflated prematurely: Future unreceived income is never added to Safe to Spend until you actually receive it and update Current Available Money (or mark the income received and add it to your balance).

How do Custom Months, Yearly, Weekly, Biweekly, and One-Time items work?

PlusMinus handles both regular bills and irregular schedules accurately across Plan and Live:

  • Plan normalization: Weekly items normalize via × 52 ÷ 12, every 2 weeks via × 26 ÷ 12, Custom Months (such as January & October or Quarterly presets) via × selected months ÷ 12, and yearly items via ÷ 12.
  • Live full deductions: Yearly and custom-month bills are deducted in full during the actual pay period when they fall due, so you see their real cash impact before payday.
  • One-Time items: One-time income and one-time bills (for example a tax refund or car repair) have an exact date and are excluded from your steady-state Plan baseline, appearing only when due in Live.
  • Categories & optional due days: Recurring bills can be organized with optional categories (Housing, Utilities, Subscriptions, Insurance, Loan & Debt, Transport, Other), and recurring items can omit a specific due day if needed.

How do Primary Payday and weekend payday shifting work?

Your Primary Payday in Settings → Budget Cycle anchors your pay cycles and food estimate resets:

  • Days 1–31 with short-month handling: Choose your main payday from day 1 to 31. In shorter months (such as February or 30-day months), PlusMinus automatically clamps the date to the last day of that month.
  • Weekend shifting: Turn on Pay on Friday if payday falls on a weekend if your employer pays on the preceding Friday whenever the payday falls on Saturday or Sunday.
  • Cycle preview: In the Primary Payday picker, you can preview upcoming effective payday dates before saving.

What are Coming Next Period, paying bills early, and older unsettled bills?

PlusMinus makes it easy to stay ahead of upcoming cycles and keep older periods tidy:

  • Coming Next Period & early payment: Expand Coming Next Period in Live to preview bills due after your next primary payday. If you already paid a next-period bill early, mark it paid there with the same balance consistency check.
  • Older unsettled bills (Mark older settled): Unpaid bills from before the previous cycle (up to 24 months old) are excluded from deducting against your current Safe to Spend and surfaced in a non-destructive catch-up banner with Mark older settled.

How are amounts rounded?

Every visible figure is designed so the arithmetic adds up clearly:

  • Cent precision & exact totals: Monetary components are rounded to the nearest cent (with half-cent ties rounding away from zero) before summing or subtracting, so every displayed line adds up to the exact total shown.
  • Food estimate rounding: The automatic daily food estimate in Live rounds up to a whole currency unit, whereas custom fixed food estimates keep cents.
  • Clean decimal display: Amounts with cents display two decimal places, while whole amounts omit trailing decimals.

Does PlusMinus connect to my bank, collect analytics, or sync automatically to the cloud?

No. PlusMinus is built strictly as a local-first, zero-tracking app:

  • Stored on your device: Balances, incomes, bills, payment history, and preferences stay locally on your iPhone or iPad.
  • No accounts or bank links: PlusMinus requires no account and never asks for bank credentials or Open Banking access.
  • Zero tracking or ads: There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no automatic cloud sync in the background.

How do Backup & Restore and Reset All Data work?

You stay in full control of your local budget data at all times:

  • Create Backup: Open Settings → Data & Privacy → Backup & Restore and tap Create Backup to export a readable JSON archive (.plusminusbackup, not password-protected) to iCloud Drive, local storage, or any location in Files.
  • Restore from Backup: Tap Restore from Backup and select a valid .plusminusbackup file (up to 50 MB). Restoring replaces all current budget data and restores the balance from when the backup was created, while keeping your app language unchanged.
  • Reset All Data: Under Settings → Data & Privacy, Reset All Data uses a two-step confirmation to erase all local budget data and preferences on this device and return to initial setup. Exported backup files and iOS system backups are not deleted.

What languages, currencies, themes, and devices are supported?

PlusMinus adapts to your preferred language, currency, and appearance:

  • Languages: Switchable directly inside Settings → Preferences → Language between System Default, English, and Suomi (Finnish) without restarting the app.
  • Currencies: Supports EUR, USD, GBP, SEK, NOK, DKK, CHF, CAD, AUD, and JPY under Settings → Preferences → Currency.
  • Appearance: Choose System Default, Dark, or Light under Settings → Preferences → Appearance.
  • Supported devices: Built for iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or later.
